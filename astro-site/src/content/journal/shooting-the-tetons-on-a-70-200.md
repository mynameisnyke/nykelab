Everyone brings the wide lens to the Tetons. I left it in the car and shot the whole trip on a 70–200, and I am not going back.

## Why long

The range is huge, but the best light is a thin stripe across a few peaks. A longer lens lets you pick the stripe and ignore the rest of the valley.

## What I would pack again

- A tripod you do not mind carrying uphill.
- A graduated filter for the first ten minutes of sunrise.
- Patience. The light moves faster than you think.
